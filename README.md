# machcrit-legal

Public legal documents for **MachCrit**, the personal pilot logbook by **Chad Clarke**, for iPhone, iPad, and Apple silicon Mac.

MachCrit is offered on the Apple App Store **worldwide except the Russian Federation and the People's Republic of China**.

| Document | URL |
|---|---|
| Privacy Policy | https://chadclarke1.github.io/machcrit-legal/PrivacyPolicy |
| Terms of Use | https://chadclarke1.github.io/machcrit-legal/TermsOfUse |

These pages are linked from the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/machcrit/id6763485825) and from the in-app purchase flow.

Also linked from the store listing: [Apple's Standard EULA](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

MachCrit Pro map display uses the [Mapbox Maps SDK](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/tos). Mapbox telemetry opt-out is described in the Privacy Policy.
