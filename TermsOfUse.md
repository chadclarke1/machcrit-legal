MachCrit Logbook is a **personal convenience tool** designed to assist pilots in recording and organising their flight activity. It is **not** certified, approved, or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), International Civil Aviation Organisation (ICAO), Civil Aviation Safety Authority (CASA), Civil Aviation Authority (CAA), South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA), or any other national or international aviation authority.

The App does not replace an official pilot logbook. You are solely responsible for maintaining flight records that meet the requirements of the applicable aviation regulations in your jurisdiction.

### 3.2 Auto-Detected Data May Contain Errors

The App uses GPS signals, speed thresholds, and altitude data to automatically detect flight phases, takeoff times, landing times, and block times. These values are **estimates** and may be inaccurate due to:
- GPS signal quality or availability
- Device sensor limitations
- Airspace or environmental conditions
- Edge cases in the detection algorithm

Always verify automatically generated flight times, distances, and computed values before:
- Submitting records to any regulatory body, employer, or examiner
- Using them as the basis for currency or recency calculations
- Including them in insurance claims or legal proceedings

### 3.3 ADS-B Enrichment Data

When the ADS-B enrichment feature is enabled (Pro subscription required), the App retrieves supplemental flight data from FlightRadar24 via a developer-operated proxy. This data — including departure and arrival airports, callsigns, and supplemental track points — is provided by a third party and **may be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect**.

The Developer makes no warranty as to the accuracy of ADS-B-derived data. Always confirm departure and arrival airports and other enriched fields against your own records.

### 3.4 Currency Calculations Are for Reference Only

Currency and recency indicators displayed in the App (medical expiry, BFR due date, instrument currency, night currency, and similar) are calculated based solely on the data you have entered. They are provided for **reference and convenience only**.

You must independently verify your own regulatory currency before acting as pilot in command or exercising any certificate privileges. The App must not be used as the sole basis for any flight safety decision.

### 3.5 Not for Operational Use in Flight

The App is a logbook and record-keeping tool. It is not designed or intended for use as a navigation aid, flight planning tool, or operational reference during flight. Do not use the App in place of certified avionics, approved charts, or authorised flight planning systems.
