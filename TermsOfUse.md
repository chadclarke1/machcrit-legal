### 3.2 Auto-Detected Data May Contain Errors

The App uses GPS signals, speed thresholds, and altitude data to automatically detect flight phases, takeoff times, landing times, and block times. These values are **estimates** and may be inaccurate due to:
- GPS signal quality or availability
- Device sensor limitations
- Airspace or environmental conditions
- Edge cases in the detection algorithm

Always verify automatically generated flight times, distances, and computed values before:
- Submitting records to any regulatory body, employer, or examiner
- Using them as the basis for currency or recency calculations
- Including them in insurance claims or legal proceedings

### 3.3 ADS-B Enrichment Data

When the ADS-B enrichment feature is enabled (Pro subscription required), the App retrieves supplemental flight data from FlightRadar24 via a developer-operated proxy. This data — including departure and arrival airports, callsigns, and supplemental track points — is provided by a third party and **may be incomplete, delayed, or incorrect**.

The Developer makes no warranty as to the accuracy of ADS-B-derived data. Always confirm departure and arrival airports and other enriched fields against your own records.

### 3.4 Currency Calculations Are for Reference Only

Currency and recency indicators displayed in the App (medical expiry, licence remaining validity, flight review or proficiency-check due dates, instrument currency, night currency, and similar indicators for supported rule sets) are calculated based solely on the data you have entered or imported. They are provided for **reference and convenience only**.

Supported rule sets are a software convenience. They may be incomplete, simplified, or out of date relative to the regulations that actually apply to you.

You must independently verify your own regulatory currency before acting as pilot in command or exercising any certificate privileges. The App must not be used as the sole basis for any flight safety or legal-to-fly decision.

### 3.5 Pilot Wallet Is Not an Official Credential

Pilot Wallet can display licence and medical details you enter, and may store images of documents you add. It is **not** an official digital licence, medical certificate, or identity document. Do not present Pilot Wallet to an inspector, examiner, or operator as a substitute for the physical or electronic credentials your authority requires.

### 3.6 Smart Assist, Smart Import & Document Assistance

Smart Assist, smart import, document-assisted workflows, flight-intelligence summaries, and similar suggestions may propose times, airports, aircraft, crew, remarks, or other fields. Those proposals can be wrong, incomplete, or misread from a scan or file.

You must review and confirm every assisted or imported value before relying on it. The Developer is not responsible for:
- OCR or language-model misreads

- Suggestions that do not match the regulations, company SOP, or training syllabus that apply to you

Do not use Smart Assist as a navigation, weather, performance, or in-flight decision tool.

### 3.7 Maps, Airports & Not for Operational Use in Flight

The App may show maps (via Apple Maps) and a local airport list, including home/base, usage, and "master match" status against a reference dataset.

The App is a logbook and record-keeping tool. It is **not** designed or intended for use as:
- A navigation aid

- A flight planning, dispatch, or operational reference during flight

Do not use the App in place of those systems. Airport coordinates, names, and radio-service labels in the App may be outdated or wrong.

### 3.8 Schedule Matching
